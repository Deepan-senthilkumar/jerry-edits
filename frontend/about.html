Our Channel Journey

Jerry Edits was founded on August 21, 2020 by Deepan S. What began as a personal passion for South Indian cinema and emotive background scores evolved into a platform celebrating the best moments of Tamil music, romantic dialogues, and memorable cinematic sequences.

Over the years, we have published over 89 curated status edits, accumulating more than 285,000 lifetime views and a growing community of 540+ dedicated subscribers across YouTube, Instagram, and Telegram.

What We Create

Every video on Jerry Edits is tailored to the exact 30-second vertical format ideal for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. You can explore and stream these cuts in our complete Videos Gallery:

  • Emotional BGM & Melodies: Heartfelt tunes celebrating nostalgic Tamil cinema scores from legendary composers like Anirudh Ravichander, Yuvan Shankar Raja, Harris Jayaraj, and A.R. Rahman.
  • Love & Romantic Edits: Visual pairings of iconic song lyrics with aesthetic typography, slow-motion sequences, and romantic scenes.
  • Friendship & Gang Edits: High-energy, celebratory status cuts capturing the spirit of youth, college memories, and brotherhood.
  • Mass EFX & Mashups: Dynamic beat-sync editing with smooth transitions, color grading presets, and bass-boosted sound design.
