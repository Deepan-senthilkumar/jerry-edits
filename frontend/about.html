Our Creative Process & Software

Crafting a 30-second status video requires precision, rhythm matching, and storytelling in a compact timeframe:

  1. Audio Selection & Beat Marking: Extracting the most emotionally resonant 30 seconds of a soundtrack and placing beat markers.
  2. Frame Trimming & Pacing: Handpicking high-definition 1080p/4K cinematic cuts that complement the lyrical message.
  3. Visual Effects (EFX): Applying subtle zoom transitions, shake effects, glow elements, and light leaks using mobile and desktop software like Alight Motion, CapCut, and Adobe Premiere Pro.
  4. Typography & Color Palette: Matching Tamil typography fonts with custom gradients to ensure crisp visibility on mobile devices.
